## Context and problem

The VAST Challenge scenario follows seven AI agents managing communication around an acquisition embargo. Merger details appeared on agent-linked social accounts roughly one hour before the permitted release.

The task was to reconstruct what happened, compare the incident with normal behaviour, and identify earlier signals that the control environment could fail.

## My role

I completed the analysis independently. I designed the data model, transformed the nested JSON, selected the visual methods, built the interactive views, and wrote the final interpretation.

## Process

I flattened the source into message-level and round-level tables, then combined five analytical views:

1. An escalation timeline
2. A communication leak chain
3. Baseline versus incident behaviour
4. Directed interaction networks
5. Within-agent anomaly signals

Several source inconsistencies required explicit handling. Recipient identifiers did not always match sender roles, sentiment used two labelling systems, and a stock-price spike appeared inconsistent with the surrounding series. I documented each issue instead of silently correcting it.

## Key finding

The investigation reframed the incident as a deliberate act enabled by systemic gaps. The compliance agent was not operating across every communication channel, which made the failure a coverage problem rather than a simple model failure.

The analysis also challenged several first-pass assumptions. The evidence did not support blaming the interns, and it did not support attributing the stock decline solely to the early release.

## Reflection

The project strengthened my ability to connect technical evidence with governance questions. Network structure, timing, and channel coverage became more useful than treating every message as an isolated record.
